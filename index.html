🎯 Project Aim

To present a comprehensive analysis and implementation of a machine learning model designed for predicting the prices of second-hand cars. By utilizing a synthetic dataset, the project explores relationships between various car features and their impact on pricing, providing accurate predictions to aid potential buyers in making informed purchasing decisions.

📄 Abstract

This project details the development of a machine learning model for predicting second-hand car prices using a synthetic dataset generated from key vehicle features. Through exploratory data analysis (EDA), significant patterns and trends influencing car pricing were identified.

Linear Regression Flask Integration Real-time Predictions Quickr Dataset
Dataset Information

Data Description

The dataset is sourced from Quickr, a popular online marketplace for automobiles.

Company (Manufacturer)

Identifies the manufacturer of the car, which significantly influences resale value due to brand reputation and reliability.

Model

The specific model plays a crucial role in price differentiation, as certain models may be more sought after than others.

Year of Purchase

The age of the car is a key determinant of value; typically, newer cars fetch higher prices compared to older models.

Fuel Type

Different fuel types (petrol, diesel, electric) impact pricing due to varying demand and operational costs associated with each type.

Kilometers Driven

Reflects vehicle usage, with higher kilometers indicating greater wear and tear, which typically lowers the car's value.
